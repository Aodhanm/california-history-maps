Ranchos of California

The ranchos now have their own research portal: ranchos.archivesofcalifornia.com holds all 673 grants and claims with their boundaries, diseños, cattle brands, family histories and land-case outcomes. This page is kept so older links keep working.

Every private land grant of Spanish and Mexican California, brought together in one place: an interactive boundary map colored by the governor who made each grant, and a searchable index of all the claims later filed with the U.S. Land Commission — each grant linked to its owner, acreage, land case, adjudication, and its original hand-drawn diseño.

The Interactive Map

Boundaries colored by granting governor, 1769–1846

511 surveyed grant boundaries across the state. Click any rancho for its governor, owner, acreage and size rank, land case, U.S. outcome, and a carousel of its diseño maps. Search a family name to light up every grant that dynasty held.

The Searchable Index

All 662 distinct grants, sortable and filterable

Every grant — mapped or not — with year, governor, grantee, acreage, county, and confirmed/rejected status. Sort any column, filter by outcome, and click a grant to jump to it on the map. The ones without a surveyed boundary link straight to their diseño.

How the pieces fit

The map shows the grants that were surveyed and given an exact boundary. The index holds the full universe of claims, so the ones that were rejected or never surveyed still appear — with their diseño where one survives. Boundaries come from the ECAI/UCSD survey reconstruction; grant records from Ogden Hoffman's Reports of Land Cases (1862) and the Bancroft land-case files. Reconstructed or contested grants (Suscol, Mare Island, Yerba Buena Island) are drawn approximately and labeled as such.
